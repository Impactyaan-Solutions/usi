─────────────────────────────────────────────
SCHEME OVERVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Palanhaar Yojana is a Rajasthan Government scheme providing monthly financial assistance for foster care and education of vulnerable children. It funds a foster parent-child unit to ensure at-risk children are raised in a family environment rather than an institution. The scheme is state-funded, uses automated DBT payments, and verifies data through government databases.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
ELIGIBLE CATEGORIES (10 categories)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

The child must be under 18 (extendable to 19 if completing Class 12) and fall into one of these categories:
1. Orphan children (both parents deceased)
2. Children of parents sentenced to death or life imprisonment
3. Children of widowed mother receiving social security pension (max 3 children)
4. Children of remarried widowed mother
5. Children of HIV/AIDS affected parents
6. Children of leprosy-affected parents
7. Children of estranged or absconded mother (max 3 children)
8. Children of specially-abled (Divyang) parents (40%+ disability)
9. Children of divorced or abandoned women receiving social security pension
10. Children of silicosis-affected parents

─────────────────────────────────────────────
FOSTER PARENT (PALANHAAR) ELIGIBILITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Must be a Rajasthan native or have resided in the state for at least three years
- Annual family income must not exceed Rs. 1,20,000
  - Income limit is WAIVED for BPL/Aastha/Antyodaya cardholders or social security pensioners (widow, specially-abled, silicosis)
- Must NOT be a government employee
- A sibling above 18 years can be Palanhaar for Categories 1, 2, 4, and 7 only

─────────────────────────────────────────────
FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Monthly Grant:
- Orphan children (Category 1):
  - Ages 0–6: Rs. 1,500/month
  - Ages 6–18: Rs. 2,500/month
- All other categories (2–10), revised from 1 July 2023:
  - Ages 0–6: Rs. 750/month
  - Ages 6–18: Rs. 1,500/month

Annual Lump Sum: Rs. 2,000 per child for books, stationery, clothes, sweater, shoes.
- NOT applicable to widow foster parents or relative foster parents.
- Application must be submitted between 1 July and 31 March to receive for current year.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Education is mandatory for children aged 3 and above:
- Ages 0–3: No attendance requirement
- Ages 3–6: Must be registered and attending Anganwadi Centre or pre-school
- Ages 6–18: Must attend school, college, or vocational/technical institution

Verification:
- Auto-verified by matching child's Aadhaar with Shala Darpan portal
- If auto-verification fails, foster parent must submit annual attendance certificate manually

Consequences of non-attendance:
- Monthly grant is suspended
- If renewal not completed by 31 December: application temporarily rejected
- If not renewed for entire academic year (July–June): benefit permanently cancelled, no backdated payments, fresh application required

Children in government/aided residential schools receiving free food, clothing, education are NOT eligible.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Mandatory for all foster parents:
- Janadhaar Card (mandatory)
- Domicile Certificate / Ration Card / Voter ID
- Income Certificate (not required for BPL/Aastha/Antyodaya cardholders or social security pensioners)

Category-specific documents:
- Orphan: Death certificates of both parents
- Incarcerated parents: Court sentence order copy
- Widowed/Divorced/Abandoned mother: Social Security Pension Payment Order (PPO)
- Remarried widow: Certificate of remarriage
- HIV/AIDS: Registration certificate with Rajasthan AIDS Control Society (Green Diary)
- Leprosy/Silicosis: Medical certificate from authorized Medical Officer
- Specially-abled parents: Disability certificate (40%+)
- Estranged/Absconded mother: Certificate from Sub-Divisional Officer

Additional:
- For Categories 1, 2, 4, 7: Certificate from Sarpanch/Ward Councillor confirming child lives with foster parent
- Child must have Aadhaar linked to foster parent's Janadhaar family ID
- Documents already on Janadhaar/DigiLocker need not be re-uploaded

─────────────────────────────────────────────
APPLICATION PROCESS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Three ways to apply:
- e-Mitra Kiosk
- SSO ID (self-application online)
- Palanhaar Mobile App (available since October 2023)

Process:
1. System auto-identifies eligible families via Janadhaar and sends SMS/voice call
2. Foster parent starts application; data pre-populated from Janadhaar
3. First-time applicants: biometric or OTP verification required
4. Upload only non-portal-certified documents (medical certificates, court orders not on Janadhaar)
5. Child's education auto-verified via Shala Darpan; manual upload needed only if auto-verification fails

MANDATORY: Child must be linked to foster parent's Janadhaar family ID before applying.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
APPROVAL TIMELINES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Officer approval: Block/District Social Justice Officer must approve within 30 days; auto-approved if no action taken
- Payment approval order: Must be issued within 15 days of approval; auto-generated if missed
- Disbursement: Initiated by second week of every month via DBT

─────────────────────────────────────────────
PAYMENT PROCESS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Centralised DBT directly to foster parent's bank account
- Processed via PFMS/Pay Manager with single Drawing Officer and single Treasury (Jaipur Rural)
- E-bills generated centrally around 7th of every month; cleared by Treasury next day
- No fixed quota; all eligible units entitled to benefits
- Partial budget: algorithm determines disbursement priority; remaining bills auto-generated when budget available

Annual lump sum of Rs. 2,000 paid alongside monthly grant after annual renewal/attendance verification.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
15TH OF MONTH CUT-OFF RULE
─────────────────────────────────────────────

This rule determines pro-rata payment for three scenarios:

1. New Applications:
   - Applied by 15th: full payment for that month
   - Applied 16th onwards: payment starts from 1st of next month

2. Age Milestones (child turns 6 — rate increase):
   - Turns 6 on or before 15th: higher rate applies that full month
   - Turns 6 after 15th: higher rate from next month

3. Benefit Cessation (child turns 18 or 19):
   - Turns 18/19 on or before 15th: no payment for that month
   - Turns 18/19 after 15th: full payment for that final month

DOB is taken from Shala Darpan (primary) → Janadhaar → Aadhaar. Manual overrides not permitted.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AGE VERIFICATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Priority order for Date of Birth:
1. Shala Darpan portal (primary)
2. Janadhaar portal
3. Aadhaar portal

Benefits calculated strictly on DOB at time of application. No manual overrides permitted.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
ANNUAL RENEWAL
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Mandatory every year to confirm foster parent's survival and child's school registration.
- Renewal window: July to December (deadline: 31 December)
- Auto-renewed if Aadhaar matches Shala Darpan; manual attendance certificate needed if not
- Missing deadline: application temporarily rejected, benefits suspended
- Not renewed for full year (July–June): benefit permanently cancelled, no backdated payments
- To restart: fresh application required; payments only from new application date

─────────────────────────────────────────────
INCOMPLETE DOCUMENTS / OBJECTIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

If officer raises an objection:
- 1st notice: 30 days to rectify
- 2nd notice: additional 30 days (45 days total)
- 3rd/final notice: additional 30 days (90 days total)
- If not resolved within 90 days: application automatically rejected

─────────────────────────────────────────────
WHEN BENEFITS ARE STOPPED OR CANCELLED
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Age/Education reasons:
- Child turns 18 (or 19 if still in Class 12)
- Child passes Class 12 (even if under 19)
- Child fails to attend Anganwadi (ages 3–6) or school (ages 6–18)

Foster parent status changes:
- Joins government service
- Income exceeds Rs. 1.2 lakh (unless BPL/pensioner exempt)
- Widowed/divorced/abandoned foster parent remarries
- Death of foster parent or child

Administrative failures:
- Annual renewal missed for full academic year (permanent cancellation)
- Objections not resolved within 90 days

Living conditions:
- Child moved to government/aided residential school (free food, clothing, education provided)
- Foster parent found to be neglecting or harming child (can be replaced)

Foster parents have legal obligation to voluntarily update Janadhaar and stop benefits when ineligible. Non-compliance may result in fund recovery.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
RESIDENTIAL SCHOOL EXCLUSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────

No benefits if child is in a government or government-aided residential school/home receiving free accommodation, food, clothing, and education.

Exception: Benefits continue if child studies in a different district/state but is NOT in a government-aided residential facility covering all needs.

Child must be living with foster parent on the date of application.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
BACKDATED PAYMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Backdated payments are NOT allowed under any circumstances after missed renewal.
- Missed renewal window (July–December): temporary rejection, benefits suspended
- Missed entire academic year (July–June): permanent cancellation, no retrospective payments
- To restart: fresh application only; payments from new application date only
