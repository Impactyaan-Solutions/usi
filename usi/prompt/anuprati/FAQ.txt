FAQ of MUKHYAMANTRI ANUPRATI COACHING YOJANA

─────────────────────────────────────────────
SCHEME OVERVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Mukhyamantri Anuprati Coaching Yojana is a Rajasthan Government scheme that provides free, quality coaching to youth from the state for competitive/professional entrance exams and government job exams. It pays the coaching fee directly to empanelled coaching institutes, and pays an additional accommodation/food/exam-fee allowance directly to eligible students via DBT. The scheme targets students from SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS, Minority, and PwD categories whose family income does not exceed ₹8 lakh per year. The scheme is administered by the Social Justice & Empowerment Department (SJED), which acts as the nodal department, with the Directorate of Special Ability (Divyangjan) and the Minority Affairs Department handling their respective category students. All applications are processed through a single online portal.

The scheme's revised guidelines (issued via circular no. 21178 dated 15.11.2024) apply from session 2024-25 onward, with immediate effect across the state.

State-level program in-charge: the Additional Director (Chhatravritti), SJED Directorate, Jaipur, is designated as the portal admin and program in-charge, responsible for overall implementation, supervision, and monitoring of the scheme statewide — including control over the portal and all its aspects.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
ELIGIBLE CATEGORIES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Eligible student categories:
1. Scheduled Caste (SC)
2. Scheduled Tribe (ST)
3. Other Backward Class (OBC)
4. Most Backward Class (MBC)
5. Economically Weaker Section (EWS)
6. Minority
7. Persons with Disabilities (PwD / Divyangjan)

⚠ ADDENDUM (proposed, status unclear — see "Open Items"): A new sub-category of 1% reserved seats is proposed for children covered under the Juvenile Justice Act — Children in Need of Care and Protection (CNCP), Children in Conflict with Law (CICL), and current/former Palanhaar Yojana beneficiaries. This is not present in the SOP or Scheme Summary and its approval/effective status is not confirmed.

Per this proposed provision: these three sub-categories would be allotted via a combined merit-cum-preference list, and — importantly — the coaching fee and hostel/food/exam-fee allowance for any beneficiary selected under this sub-category would be paid from the budget head of that beneficiary's own original category/class (e.g., an SC-category CNCP child's benefit is still paid from the SC budget), not from a separate pooled budget.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
STUDENT ELIGIBILITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Must be a domicile/original resident of Rajasthan
- Must belong to one of the eligible categories above
- Family annual income must not exceed ₹8,00,000 (including the student's own income, if any)
- Must not have previously availed the scheme (auto-checked by the portal — meaning the student was previously selected and had already joined a coaching institute under the scheme in an earlier session)
- Student must not be a serving regular government employee (central/state/PSU/board/any statutory government body)
- Student's self-declaration checkbox is mandatory — application cannot be submitted without it, confirming all uploaded facts/documents are complete and true

─────────────────────────────────────────────
MERIT CALCULATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Merit is determined based on marks obtained in the minimum qualifying exam (10th or 12th board), category-wise and exam-wise.
- For JEE/NEET, CLAT, and CA FC/CS EET/CMA FC + CUET coaching, merit is based on 10th board marks.
- For all other listed exams — UPSC Civil Services, RAS & Subordinate Service Combined Competitive Exam (RPSC), Sub-Inspector & Pay Matrix Level-10+ exams (RPSC), REET, RSSB exams (Patwari, Junior Assistant, etc.), Banking/Insurance, Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), and CDS/SSC — merit is based on 12th board marks.
- For determining merit, the percentage awarded by CBSE and Other Boards in the 10th or 12th board examination shall be multiplied by a factor of 0.9, while the percentage obtained in RBSE Board's 10th/12th shall be retained as it is.
- If two or more candidates have the same qualifying percentage, the older candidate is given preference in the merit order.
- Coaching institute allotment uses a Merit-cum-Preference model: the candidate's filled preference order of coaching institutes (mandatory at application) is combined with their merit rank to allot an institute.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
APPLICATION PROCESS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

- Applications are made via e-Mitra / self-application using SSO ID, through the departmental portal, once the EOI/notification is released.
- Data is auto-verified/fetched from Jan Aadhaar wherever possible; only data not available via web services must be manually entered/uploaded.
- Students must select and rank their coaching institute preference order for each course/exam at the time of application — this is mandatory. Coaching institute allotment is based on Merit-cum-Preference within the state-level targets fixed per category.
- A student can select only one exam category per session ("one student, one exam" rule).
- Once a student joins an allotted coaching institute, they are not eligible to re-apply for the scheme in a later session — this is auto-flagged by the portal.
- For two-year courses (Engineering/Medical), only one merit list is issued for the full duration; a separate merit list is not issued for online joining in the second year.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
SELECTION PIPELINE
─────────────────────────────────────────────

| Step | Stage | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Student Application | Student applies on portal with documents and coaching institute preferences |
| 2 | Auto-Verification | Caste, domicile, marksheets auto-verified via Jan Aadhaar / Raj e-Vault / DigiLocker / Board database |
| 3 | Provisional Merit List | Published per exam category, per category |
| 4 | District Verification | District officer verifies documents of listed students within 10 days; publishes corrected merit |
| 5 | Main Merit List | Verified list published; students must join coaching institute within 10 days or seat is forfeited |
| 6 | 2nd Counselling Round | Forfeited seats and remaining seats allocated via 2nd round based on merit cum preference |
| 7 | Final List | Consolidated final list of selected students and assigned institutes |
| 8 | Waiting List | If a student drops out mid-course, next eligible student on waiting list gets the seat (same session only) |

─────────────────────────────────────────────
PAYMENT STRUCTURE
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Coaching fee is paid to the institute, not the student. Only the accommodation/food/exam-fee allowance is paid directly to the student via DBT.

COACHING FEE — THREE INSTALLMENTS TO THE INSTITUTE

| Installment | Timing | Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | After joining (Aadhaar biometric/OTP) + 30 days of actual regular attendance, online-verified | 60% | Attendance verified via biometric/OTP on portal |
| 2nd | After completion of full course/exam syllabus | 35% | Portal-verified process completion; for UPSC/RPSC combined Pre+Mains courses, a self-attested certificate of full syllabus coverage is required before 2nd installment |
| 3rd | After exam result / hall ticket verification | 5% | District Officer verifies hall ticket and result upload on the portal; disbursed only if the coached beneficiary meets the minimum result-percentage benchmark |


ACCOMMODATION / FOOD / EXAM-FEE ALLOWANCE

- Students are paid ₹40,000/year which covers accommodation, food and exam fees for eligible relocating students.
- Amount: ₹40,000/year, disbursed quarterly by the District Officer (of the institute's district)
- Only for students who must relocate away from their home city for the allotted coaching
- Requires a self-declared, coaching-institute-countersigned affidavit as proof of rental/relocation (as per addendum 12-A)
- Students residing in departmental hostels are NOT eligible for this allowance
- The institute must upload the relevant beneficiary documents under the "Hostel Allowance" option on the portal
