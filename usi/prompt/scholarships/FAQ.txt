RAJASTHAN POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP – KNOWLEDGE BASE

─────────────────────────────────────────────
ELIGIBILITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────

A student can apply if:
- They are a bonafide resident of Rajasthan.
- They belong to an eligible category (SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EBC, DNT or other scheme-specific categories including Mirasi community and students in national institutions like IITs/IIMs under Mukhyamantri Sarvjan Uchcha Shiksha Chhatravriti Yojna).
- They are studying after Class 12.
- They satisfy scheme-specific eligibility conditions.

A student is NOT eligible if:
- Admitted through Management Quota, NRI Quota or Spot Admission.
- Repeating a degree at the same academic level (e.g. BA after BCom).
- Switching professional streams after completing one (e.g. B.Ed after LLB).
- Academic gap exceeds 4 years from the last qualifying examination.
- More than one scholarship application is submitted in the same academic session.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
APPLICATION PROCESS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Applications can only be submitted through:
- SSO Portal: sso.rajasthan.gov.in
- SJMS Scholarship Portal: sjmsnew.rajasthan.gov.in/scholarship
- SJED Mobile App: SJED APPLICATIONS (available on app stores)

Requirements:
- Aadhaar-linked SSO ID
- Jan Aadhaar
- Aadhaar-based biometric authentication (fingerprint/iris) or face authentication via mobile app

Paper applications are not accepted.
Both fresh and renewal applications are allowed.
Only one application is permitted per academic session.
Once submitted, the selected institution cannot be changed.

Current application deadline: 30 June 2026.
Check official portals regularly for deadline extensions or updates.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
─────────────────────────────────────────────

The system automatically fetches most data through Jan Aadhaar, DigiLocker and Raj e-Vault.

Students may need:
- Aadhaar
- Jan Aadhaar
- Domicile Certificate
- Caste Certificate
- 10th and 12th Marksheets
- Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account

Students from Rajasthan Board and Central Boards usually do not need to upload marksheets manually.
Students from other boards may need to upload marksheets.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AADHAAR REQUIREMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Aadhaar is mandatory for:
- SSO Login
- e-KYC
- Biometric Authentication
- Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

Applications cannot be submitted without Aadhaar authentication.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────

What is the last date to apply?
The current deadline for submitting applications is 30th June 2026. Check for new circulars in scholarship website in case of any extensions to this deadline.

Attendance is recorded through Aadhaar-based biometric systems maintained by institutions on a monthly basis.

Students enrolled in IGNOU or VMOU distance education programs are exempt from biometric attendance requirements.

Failure to maintain the required attendance may result in stoppage of scholarship benefits.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
INSTITUTION VERIFICATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────

The institution verifies:
- Student details
- Documents
- Attendance
- Fee structure
- Bank account details

Only approved institutions can process scholarship applications.
Institutions must renew their registration every academic year.

If a Nodal Officer changes:
- The institution must submit verified documents to the District Officer.
- The District Officer uploads the documents and maps the new Nodal Officer's SSO ID on the portal.
- All changes are recorded in the audit trail.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
SCHOLARSHIP PAYMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Scholarship is disbursed through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

Students must ensure:
- Account is active.
- KYC is completed.
- Aadhaar is seeded with the account.
- No transaction limits prevent receipt of funds.
- Minor accounts receiving more than ₹25,000 are converted to major accounts.

Once scholarship funds are credited, the student must pay pending institutional fees within 7 days.
This is especially relevant for students admitted using Free-ship Card provisions.

If a payment fails due to bank issues, the student will receive an alert and must visit the district office within the specified timeframe to update account details.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
AADHAAR SEEDING
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Aadhaar can be linked to a bank account through:
- Net Banking: Log in, go to Profile or Services, select Update/Link Aadhaar, enter 12-digit Aadhaar number and submit.
- Mobile Banking App: Open app, go to Services or Requests, select Link Aadhaar, enter details and submit.
- Bank Branch Visit: Fill Aadhaar seeding consent form (Annexure I) and submit a self-attested copy of Aadhaar card.

Required documents:
- 12-digit Aadhaar Number
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank Passbook

Seeding typically takes 1–5 business days to reflect.
The bank links the account to the NPCI mapper used for DBT payments.
Status can be checked at NPCI website using the Bharat Aadhaar Seeding Enabler (BASE) tool.

If account is not seeded:
- The institution's Scholarship Nodal Officer will flag this during verification.
- The student has 15 days to get the account linked and seeded.
- If payment is rejected due to seeding issues, the student must visit the district office to update bank account details.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
OBJECTIONS AND CORRECTIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────

If an issue is found during verification, the application status becomes "Back to Student".

The student receives 45 days to correct and resubmit the application using Aadhaar authentication.

Failure to resolve objections within 45 days results in automatic rejection.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
RED FLAG CASES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

A Red Flag may be triggered if important details are changed after application submission, including:
- Name
- Date of Birth
- Caste
- Bank Details

Consequences:
- Additional scrutiny and manual dual verification
- Visible to institution and district-level officials
- Delay in bill generation and payment
- Possible rejection if discrepancies cannot be justified

─────────────────────────────────────────────
GAP YEAR RULES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Students remain eligible if the gap between their last qualifying examination and current application year does not exceed 4 years.

A gap exceeding 4 years results in ineligibility.

This is checked as a standard part of Level 2 scrutiny at the District Office.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
STUDENTS STUDYING OUTSIDE RAJASTHAN
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Rajasthan residents studying in IITs, IIMs, National Institutes or Government institutions outside Rajasthan can apply for the scholarship.

The application is processed by the district office corresponding to the student's permanent residence in Rajasthan.

All Aadhaar, attendance, authentication and DBT requirements continue to apply.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
REJECTION AND REVIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Applications may be rejected if:
- Eligibility conditions are not met.
- Objections are not resolved within 45 days.
- Mandatory authentication or documentation is missing.

If rejected by the District Level Officer, the Directorate of SJE has the authority to restore or revert the decision based on merit, typically on recommendation from the District Level Officer.

For rejections caused by technical portal issues, the District Office can refer the case to the IT branch at the Directorate level with supporting evidence.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
INCOME CERTIFICATE ISSUE
─────────────────────────────────────────────

If there is an issue related to the Income Certificate:
- Income details of all members included in the Jan Aadhaar must be updated for the year for which the application is being made.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
PARTIAL PAYMENT ISSUE
─────────────────────────────────────────────

If 40% of the scholarship has been received and 60% is remaining but the Data Share Report shows status as fail:
- The student must ensure that the bank account is Aadhaar-seeded.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
PENALTIES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Providing false information, forged documents or fraudulent claims may result in:
- Scholarship rejection
- Blacklisting for 5 years
- Legal action including FIR

─────────────────────────────────────────────
COMMON TECHNICAL ERRORS AND FIXES
─────────────────────────────────────────────

Error: "Captured Success" but "Authentication Fail"
Fix: Ensure Nodal Officer's SSO ID and device are registered on aadhaarauth.rajasthan.gov.in. Verify Aadhaar number, Jan Aadhaar number and Nodal Officer details in SSO profile.

Error: Error while entering Unique Student ID in credentials
Fix: Get a new SSO ID mapped and update Aadhaar number and Jan Aadhaar number in it.

Error: Submit button not visible, only Close button appears
Fix: Check and update Jan Aadhaar details. Ensure name matches exactly across Jan Aadhaar, Domicile Certificate and Caste Certificate.

Error: Issue related to Income Certificate
Fix: Update income details of all Jan Aadhaar family members for the current application year.

Error: "bank name cannot be empty"
Fix: Update bank details in Jan Aadhaar.

Error: OTP not received during Jan Aadhaar verification
Fix: Check SSO profile and update Aadhaar number or Jan Aadhaar number if missing. Mobile number update is not required.

Error: "you are not eligible for apply scholarship application"
Fix: If the candidate belongs to SBC category but mistakenly selected OBC scheme, select the correct scheme and resubmit.

Error: "you cannot submit the application because you previously applied in some other category and are now applying in a different category"
Fix: The student had previously applied under a different category. Category cannot be changed mid-session.

Error: Wrong college or course selected after form submission
Fix: Raise an objection on the form. During objection clearance, it can be updated once.

Error: "Aadhaar number in income certificate is already mapped with another family member"
Fix: Another Jan Aadhaar family member has already applied. If that member has been split from Jan Aadhaar, they must update the new income person's Aadhaar number in their scholarship profile before the form can be filled.

Error: Wrong category selected during profile creation (e.g. BPL instead of APL)
Fix: Update the correct category in Jan Aadhaar, then go to View and Update Profile in the scholarship section to refresh the category.

Error: 40% scholarship received, 60% pending, Data Share Report shows fail
Fix: Ensure the bank account is Aadhaar-seeded.
