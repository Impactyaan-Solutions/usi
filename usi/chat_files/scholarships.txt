🎓 Top Rajasthan Government Scholarship Schemes

Chief Minister Higher Education Scholarship Scheme
– For meritorious students after Class 12 with family income criteria (e.g., up to ₹2.50 L).

Devnarayan Girls Student Scholarship / Incentive Scheme
– Financial support & incentives for female students at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

Devnarayan Girls Scooter Distribution Scheme
– Free scooty for eligible girls in certain schemes.

Meritorious Girls Student Scooty Distribution Scheme
– Scooty for meritorious female students.

Nishulk Uchh Shiksha Yojna (Free Higher Education Scheme)
– Fee support for higher education for eligible students.

Widow/Divorcee Chief Minister (B.Ed) Sambal Scheme
– Support for widow/divorcee women pursuing B.Ed education.

📘 Caste & Community-Based Scholarships

Post-Matric Scholarship (Uttar Matric) – SC, ST, OBC, SBC, EBC & DNT categories
– Financial assistance across various courses after Class 10.

Dr. Ambedkar Post-Matric Scholarship (for EBC students)
– Aid for economically backward class students.

Economic Help to Tribal Students (incl. NEET/JEE Coaching)
– For tribal students and tribal girls’ education & competitive exam support.

📚 Other Notable Schemes

Anuprati Scheme
– Entrance support for minority students (for engineering, medical, UPSC/RPSC exams, etc.).

Post-Matric Central Sector Scholarships (via National Scholarship Portal)
– Central assistance for eligible students studying higher education (often applicable to Rajasthan students).

Ambedkar Fellowship Scheme (for SC category PhD students)
– Fellowship support for postgraduate SC students undertaking PhD programmes.

Scholarship & Scooty Combo Schemes
– Special combined programmes offering scholarship plus scooter support for eligible girls.
